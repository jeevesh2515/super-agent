03 / Design principles

Permission has
a boundary.

The project is being built around three rules for delegating work by voice.

01 /

Show the scope.

Give each job a clear objective and explicit limits on what it can access or change.

⌗
02 /

Keep stop close.

Make it clear whether you are stopping speech, microphone input, or the job itself.

⊡
03 /

Leave a receipt.

Show what ran, what changed, and how the job ended so you can check the result.

▤

04 / Project notes

Good to know.

Can I use Super Agent today?

The repo includes a local voice scaffold and setup instructions. Background job delegation and the controls shown here are still being developed. Check the project roadmap ↗

What data does this page use?

The walkthrough uses two fictional build reports. It has no account, microphone access, analytics, or connection to the assistant runtime.

What does local-first mean here?

The project aims to keep control and job state on your device. Voice data handling depends on which adapter you configure; an opted-in cloud adapter may send audio to its provider. Read the architecture ↗

Where can I follow development?

The repository carries the source, design decisions, and current evidence. Open the repository ↗
